Eight-week progression

Repeat the routine regularly. The aim is better control and range over time — not to push deeper every day.

What we're targeting
  • Hamstrings and posterior chain — toe-touch difficulty
  • Hips and adductors — pigeon and deep squat positions
  • Ankles — heels lifting in a squat
  • Shoulders and upper back — overhead and kipping positions
  • Getting down to and up from the floor — burpee comfort
Baseline & retests

Measure, don't guess.

Record these every two weeks. Keep the setup the same each time so you can compare fairly.

Knee-to-wall ankle test

Barefoot, face a wall. Keep your heel flat and drive your knee to touch the wall over your toes. Move your foot back until you find the furthest distance you can reach without the heel lifting. Measure from big toe to wall.

Simple movement check-ins

Record how each feels, without forcing the position.

For your heel-lift check: stand facing a wall, feet shoulder-width, toes a short distance from it. Squat slowly while keeping heels down. If they lift, move feet slightly farther from the wall and note the distance/position that works.
